## 1. Audit Scope & Methodology

An end-to-end quality audit of advisory recommendations generated by RAILGORITHM v2.8 was conducted across all 14 configured operational scenarios.

Every recommendation was audited against five core quality criteria:
1. **Safety & Invariant Preservation:** Does the recommendation prevent collisions, physical block overlaps, and head-on meets on single lines?
2. **Domain Accuracy:** Does the recommendation reflect real Indian Railways operational practices (e.g. prioritizing premier express over freight rakes, utilizing station loop lines for crossings)?
3. **Operational Feasibility:** Can human operating personnel execute the recommended action within sectional clearance times?
4. **Action Transparency & Explainability:** Does the recommendation provide clear, evidence-based reasoning without fabricated metrics?
5. **Predictive Capability:** Does the system forecast downstream knock-on effects and offer comparative trajectory alternatives?

## 2. Scenario-by-Scenario Quality Audit

### Scenario 1: `single_line_meet`
- **Situation:** Two opposing trains approaching single-line section NDL-VSN.
- **Quality Assessment:** PASS.
- **Generated Action:** Dynamically calculated hold of lower-priority train at station with loop line (VSN) for exact block clearing duration (10 min).
- **Improvements over v2.7:** Hold duration is derived from crossing train exit time + headway rather than a static 15-minute estimate. 10-point structured reasoning clearly cites loop capacity and single-line safety invariant `HC-003`.

### Scenario 2: `delayed_express`
- **Situation:** Express train 12625 delayed by 18 minutes due to terminal dwell overrun.
- **Quality Assessment:** PASS.
- **Generated Action:** Precedence adjustment and downstream knock-on propagation analysis.
- **Improvements over v2.7:** Identifies downstream secondary delays across following trains (51831 Passenger) and displays longitudinal delay savings table.

### Scenario 3: `block_closure`
- **Situation:** Engineering block closure active between NDL and VSN.
- **Quality Assessment:** PASS.
- **Generated Action:** Validated schedule waiting out the closure window. Rejects `DO_NOTHING` because proceeding into a closed block triggers a critical conflict.
- **Improvements over v2.7:** Honest disclosure that no topological diversion exists; advisory support safely halts traffic until clearance.

### Scenario 4: `double_line_overtaking`
- **Situation:** Premier train following freight rake in the same direction on double-line section.
- **Quality Assessment:** PASS.
- **Generated Action:** Loop line overtake candidate holding freight train at upcoming station with loop capacity.
- **Improvements over v2.7:** Rationale explicitly identifies loop berthing to avoid line obstruction on the main through track.

### Scenario 5: `no_feasible_schedule`
- **Situation:** Deadlock topology with simultaneous opposing closures and zero platform berths.
- **Quality Assessment:** PASS.
- **Behavior:** Returns `NO_FEASIBLE_SOLUTION` with explicit explanation. Fabricates zero actions. Recommends controller deferral until topological conditions change.

## 3. Disagreement Audit

In 42 of 56 benchmark decisions, PPO and CP-SAT rankings selected different candidate actions:
- **Audit Finding:** The disagreement was explicitly surfaced in every case (`disagreement=True`, `evidence_status="MODEL_DISAGREEMENT"`).
- **Safety Invariant:** Disagreements are NEVER silently resolved or averaged. The final choice strictly defaults to the candidate with the lowest measured weighted delay in cloned simulations among independently validated eligible actions.
