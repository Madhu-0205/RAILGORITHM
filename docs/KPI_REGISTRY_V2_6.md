# RAILGORITHM v2.6 — Formal KPI Registry & Metric Provenance

## 1. Governance Principles

Every operational metric in RAILGORITHM v2.6 complies with the following scientific and operational standards:

1. **Backend Single Source of Truth:** All mathematical computations are executed authoritatively by `backend/app/services/analytics_service.py` and `backend/app/domain/state.py`. The frontend UI consumes backend results directly and never recalculates or approximates metrics.
2. **Epistemic Classification System:** Every metric is explicitly tagged with its certainty level:
   - `MEASURED`: Directly acquired from field track sensors, axle counters, or official station registers.
   - `CALCULATED`: Deterministically computed via mathematical formula from measured variables.
   - `PREDICTED`: Projected future states generated by timetable simulation or CP-SAT solver.
   - `HEURISTIC`: Advisory approximations based on operational rule-of-thumb models (e.g. Delay Propagation Index).
   - `NO_DATA`: Window has zero observations; displayed as `"—"`, never coerced into `0` or `100%`.
   - `STALE`: Data heartbeat older than operational freshness timeout ($> 30\text{ seconds}$).
3. **Transparent Traceability:** Every metric documents its reporting window, mathematical formula, physical unit, and regulatory authority.

## 4. Statutory vs Administrative Regulatory Basis

### Statutory Basis (Absolute Safety Rules):
- **Indian Railways General Rules (GR) 1.02(62), 8.01, 8.03(1):** Absolute Block System principles and Line Clear granting conditions. A block section must remain unoccupied until the preceding train with its tail lamp/board has passed the block clearing point.
- **Enforcement:** Enforced as a hard mathematical constraint in `backend/app/domain/validator.py`. A violation cannot be waived or overridden.

### Administrative Authority (Operating Performance Targets):
- **Railway Board Operating Directorate Guidelines:** The 5-minute punctuality tolerance ($\le 5\text{ min}$) for Mail/Express trains is an administrative monitoring target, not a statutory General Rule.
- **Throat Capacity Limits:** The 2-movement simultaneous capacity for Malgaon diamond crossing reflects local Station Working Rules (SWR), modeled explicitly as a resource constraint.
