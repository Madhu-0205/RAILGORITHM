# RAILGORITHM v2.7 — Section Controller Baseline Gap Analysis

**Release:** v2.7  
**Mission:** Operational Control Office Excellence  
**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Baseline Starting Point:** Verified v2.6 Release (`git checkout v2.7`)  

---

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive baseline audit of the existing RAILGORITHM system was conducted across both the frontend (`React + TypeScript`) and backend (`FastAPI + SQLAlchemy + OR-Tools + PyTorch`). 

### Key Baseline Findings:
1. **Monolithic Information Overload:** The previous Section Controller dashboard crammed all operational monitoring and decision-making into a single scrolling page with an excessive vertical scroll height of **11,043 pixels**. This overwhelmed the operator, required endless scrolling to find urgent information, and failed to reflect standard Indian Railways Control Office division of work.
2. **Hidden Critical Telemetry:** The initial audit revealed a runtime `TypeError` in `analytics_service.py` where trains with `entry_min: None` triggered HTTP 500 on `/analytics/section-controller`, preventing KPI card rendering in unhandled edge cases. (Now hardened).
3. **Fragmented Workspaces vs. Unified Navigation:** Key operational functions such as Time-Distance Master Graph, Station Interlocking Oversight, Absolute Block Occupancy, Train Movement Ledger, Crossings & Precedence, Engineering Caution Orders (TSR/PSR), and Shift Handover protocols lacked dedicated, accessible workspaces and coherent navigation.
4. **Information Architecture Gap:** The system lacked the 13 dedicated, high-density operational views and workspaces required by a Divisional Train Traffic Controller to supervise 5 stations and 4 connecting block sections with high situational awareness.

## Architecture Transformation Proposal

To satisfy the 13 dedicated operational views while maintaining clean modular code:
1. **Unified Operational Navigation Shell (`Shell.tsx`):**
   - Provide persistent, organized navigation grouped into logical control room operations:
     - **Core Console:** Control Overview (`/`), Master Control Graph (`/graph`), Station Control (`/stations`), Block Section Board (`/blocks`).
     - **Traffic Regulation:** Train Movements (`/trains`), Crossings & Precedence (`/crossings`), Conflicts & Alerts (`/conflicts`), AI Recommendations (`/recommendations`).
     - **Operations & Safety:** Engineering Restrictions (`/restrictions`), Incidents (`/incidents`), Traffic Analytics (`/analytics`).
     - **Governance & Continuity:** Shift Handover (`/handover`), Audit & Event History (`/audit`), Assumptions & Constraints (`/specifications`).
2. **Dedicated Workspace Pages (`frontend/src/pages/`):**
   - `MasterConsole.tsx` (or enhanced `Dashboard.tsx` focused on high-level operational overview).
   - `MasterControlGraphPage.tsx` (`/graph`): Full-viewport string chart with interactive inspection tools.
   - `StationControlPage.tsx` (`/stations`): Complete multi-station yard & berth supervision console.
   - `BlockSectionBoardPage.tsx` (`/blocks`): High-information Absolute Block matrix and bottleneck queues.
   - `CrossingsPage.tsx` (`/crossings`): Single-line meet-and-pass regulation workspace.
   - `RestrictionsPage.tsx` (`/restrictions`): TSR/PSR caution register and maintenance block board.
   - `IncidentsPage.tsx` (`/incidents`): Real-time railway incident logbook.
   - `HandoverPage.tsx` (`/handover`): Structured Section Controller shift transfer desk.
   - Existing pages (`TrainsPage`, `ConflictsPage`, `RecommendationsPage`, `AnalyticsPage`, `AuditPage`) updated to adhere to the control office theme and information density standards.
3. **Responsive High-Density UI Standards:**
   - Designed for 1440px and 1920px control room monitors.
   - Dark mode and high-contrast railway signaling palette (`#0F172A` header, clear emerald `#10B981` line clear, amber `#F59E0B` caution/TSR, crimson `#EF4444` danger/occupied, sapphire `#2563EB` accent).
   - Zero horizontal overflow, compact tabular typography with monospace numerals.
